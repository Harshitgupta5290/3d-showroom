Under the hood

Technical choices that
make the difference

Every visual effect is a deliberate engineering decision. Here's what's running.

✨
UnrealBloom Post-Processing
Three.js EffectComposer with UnrealBloomPass. Particles glow like real neon lights — bright cores with soft halos that spill across the scene. One pass transforms the entire visual quality.
🖐️
AI Hand Tracking
Google MediaPipe Hands ML model running entirely in the browser. 21 landmarks per hand at 30fps. No backend, no data leaves the device. Open palm expands. Closed fist contracts. Pinch cycles colors.
⚡
GPU Cursor Physics
Cursor repulsion and attraction computed in the GLSL vertex shader — not on the CPU. All 15,000 particles respond with quadratic falloff. Zero JavaScript overhead. Pure GPU.
🎨
Per-Particle HSL Variation
Every particle gets a unique hue, saturation, and lightness offset from the base color. 15,000 particles × individual color = a living, organic cloud that breathes instead of a flat monochrome blob.
🌌
9 Mathematical Shapes
From algebraic heart surfaces to Archimedean spiral galaxies, double helices, and vortex cones. Every transition scatters particles outward then reforms — cinematic every single time.
📁
Single File. Zero Build.
HTML, CSS, GLSL shaders, application logic, post-processing — all in one file. No npm, no webpack, no config. Open in any browser. Deploy in 60 seconds. Sell as a Gumroad product.

Three ways to play

Works the way
you work

Camera optional. Mouse and touch are first-class citizens.

🖱️
Cursor Control
Move your cursor and 15,000 particles flee it — GPU-accelerated repulsion with quadratic falloff. Click and hold to flip to attraction mode: particles rush toward you.
Move → Repel Click → Attract 1–9 shapes S screenshot F fullscreen
🖐️
AI Hand Tracking
Enable your webcam and LUMINA reads your hand using MediaPipe's ML model. Open palm expands the cloud. Close your fist to contract it. Pinch to cycle through 9 color presets.
Open Palm → Expand Closed Fist → Contract Pinch → Color
📱
Touch & Mobile
Full touch support on mobile and tablet. Drag to repel, tap-hold to attract. Pinch-to-zoom orbits the 3D scene. Works in mobile Chrome and Safari at 60fps.
