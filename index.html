Under the hood

Technical details that
make the difference

Every visual effect is a deliberate engineering choice. Here's what's running.

✨
UnrealBloom Post-Processing
Three.js EffectComposer with UnrealBloomPass. Particles glow like real neon lights — bright cores with soft halos that spill across the scene. This one pass transforms the entire visual quality.
🖐️
AI Hand Tracking
Google MediaPipe Hands ML model running entirely in the browser. 21 landmarks per hand at 30fps. No backend, no data leaves the device. Open palm expands. Closed fist contracts. Pinch cycles colors.
⚡
GPU Cursor Physics
Cursor repulsion and attraction is computed in the GLSL vertex shader — not on the CPU. All 15,000 particles respond to your cursor position with quadratic falloff. Zero JavaScript overhead.
🎨
Per-Particle HSL Variation
Every particle gets a unique hue, saturation, and lightness offset from the base color. 15,000 particles × individual color = a living, organic cloud that breathes instead of a flat monochrome blob.
🌌
9 Mathematical Shapes
From the algebraic heart surface equation to Archimedean spiral galaxies, double helices, and vortex cones. Every shape transition scatters particles outward then reforms — cinematic every time.
📁
Single File. Zero Build.
Everything — HTML, CSS, GLSL shaders, application logic, post-processing — lives in one file. No npm, no webpack, no config. Open in any browser. Deploy in 60 seconds. Sell as a Gumroad product.

Three ways to play

Works the way
you work

Camera optional. Mouse and touch are first-class citizens.

🖱️
Cursor Control
Move your cursor and 15,000 particles flee it in real-time — GPU-accelerated repulsion with quadratic falloff. Click and hold to flip to attraction mode: particles rush toward your cursor.
Move → Repel Click → Attract 1–9 shapes S screenshot F fullscreen
🖐️
AI Hand Tracking
Enable your webcam and LUMINA reads your hand in real-time using MediaPipe's ML model. Open your palm — cloud expands. Close your fist — it contracts. Pinch to cycle through 9 color presets.
Open Palm → Expand Closed Fist → Contract Pinch → Color
📱
Touch & Mobile
Full touch support on mobile and tablet. Drag your finger to repel particles, tap and hold to attract. Pinch-to-zoom orbits the 3D scene. Works in mobile Chrome and Safari at 60fps.
Drag → Repel Hold → Attract Pinch-zoom → Orbit
